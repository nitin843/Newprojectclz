Four schools, one shared curriculum.

Every student takes the same core seminars, then builds a major in the school that fits them best. You can change your path until the end of second year.

School of Sciences

Lab work begins in the first term. Students join faculty research teams and present findings at our annual symposium.

  • Biology and Environmental ScienceBSc, 4 years
  • Computer ScienceBSc, 4 years
  • Physics and MathematicsBSc, 4 years
  • Public HealthBSc, 4 years

School of Humanities

Small seminars of twelve or fewer. Every major ends with a year-long thesis guided by a faculty mentor.

  • History and PoliticsBA, 4 years
  • Literature and WritingBA, 4 years
  • PhilosophyBA, 4 years
  • Languages and CulturesBA, 4 years

School of Business

Case study teaching paired with a paid placement in each of the final two years, with partner firms in the region.

  • Management and LeadershipBBA, 4 years
  • Finance and EconomicsBBA, 4 years
  • EntrepreneurshipBBA, 4 years
  • Hospitality and TourismBBA, 4 years

School of Design and Arts

Open studios run late every day. Students exhibit work publicly each year in our campus gallery.

  • Visual CommunicationBFA, 4 years
  • Architecture StudiesBA, 4 years
  • Digital MediaBFA, 4 years
  • Music and PerformanceBFA, 4 years

A college sized for conversation.

With fewer than 1,800 students, every classroom is a discussion and every professor knows your name. Our open curriculum rewards curiosity, and our advisers help you turn it into a plan.

Scholarships cover full tuition for one in four incoming students, awarded on merit and need.
